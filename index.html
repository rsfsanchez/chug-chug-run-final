HOW TO PLAY

Collect water, keep moving, and help fill the game’s community tank.

Move
Hold A / D or ← / → (Left / Right Arrow).
Jump
Press Space or ↑ (Up Arrow). Press again in the air to double jump.
Slide
Hold S or ↓ (Down Arrow) while on the ground.

On mobile, use the arrow, Jump, and Slide buttons.

  • Collect the blue water drops.
  • Keep up! Once your entire collision body leaves the left edge of the screen, move right to return within four seconds. Reaching 1.5 meters (about five feet) beyond that edge ends the run immediately, even with hearts or damage protection left. Anywhere on-screen is safe.
  • Avoid toxic hazards and dangerous obstacles. Jump over ground hazards and slide under overhead barriers.
  • Every five water pickups provides one automatic hydration sip. The remaining water contributes toward the community water tank.
